Researcher at [Scientific Computing Laboratory](https://www.scl.rs) (SCL), [Institute of Physics Belgrade](https://www.ipb.ac.rs/en/home-en/), Serbia.

My work lies at the intersection of statistical physics, complex systems, and network science. Combining theoretical and data-driven approaches, I focus on understanding how people interact and exchange information in digital environments (including interactions with LLMs), how collective phenomena emerge in complex systems, and what drives the dynamics and sustainability of online communities. 

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Social Physics and Complexity Laboratory (SPAC) at LIP in Portugal, where I investigated biases and the spread of misinformation in online systems. I hold degree from the Faculty of Physics, University of Belgrade, and I completed PhD in complex systems at the Scientific Computing Laboratory (SCL), Institute of Physics Belgrade, in 2023.
